TYPING HERO

Typing Hero menu

Rock the keyboard. Learn to type with every finger.

This game needs a physical keyboard. On a tablet, connect one to play.

How to play

  1. Letters fall down the stage toward the glowing pads.
  2. Each lane is a finger. The colour and the label under each pad tell you which finger to use.
  3. Press the matching key as the letter reaches the pad. Right key, right moment = a hit!
  4. Build a streak to raise your multiplier. A wrong key breaks it, so accuracy comes first.
  5. Gold letters fill the Spark meter. Press Enter when it is half full for double points.
  6. Keep the crowd meter up. Press Esc to pause any time.

Typing tips

  • Rest your fingers on A S D F and J K L ;. Feel the bumps on F and J.
  • After every key, bring the finger back home.
  • Look at the screen, not your hands. Slow and correct beats fast and messy.
